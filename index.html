Why Fam Ride

Made for small towns and families

Where the big ride apps don't come, Fam Ride brings known, verified local drivers, simple booking in Hindi or English, and safety that keeps your family in the loop.

Verified drivers

Only yellow-plate vehicles. Every driver's licence, RC, insurance, permit and ID are checked by FamTap before they can take rides.

Fare before you book

See the exact fare by distance before you confirm. No surge pricing, no bargaining. Pay by UPI QR or cash.

Just say where

Speak or type in Hindi or English: "mujhe Munger Fort jaana hai" or "bus stand se hospital". Or pick any spot on the map.

Know when it comes

See how many minutes away a vehicle is before you book, and watch your driver arrive live on Google Maps.

Book for family

Book a ride for Nani or your child from anywhere. The driver picks up the person riding, and you follow the trip.

Now or later

Ride right away, or book from 10 minutes to a week ahead. Your rides of the last 30 days are in "My rides".

How it works

Book in four simple steps

Booking is a short chat. Every answer stays on screen, and you can tap any answer to change it.

  1. Where from, where toUse your current location, choose on the map, search a place, or just say it.
    “Station se Munger Fort”
  2. When and whoNow or later, for you or someone else.
  3. Pick a vehicleAuto or e-rickshaw, each with its fare and how many minutes away it is.
  4. Confirm and rideA nearby driver accepts. Give them your 4-digit start code when you get in.
Safety first

Your family rides with you

Fam Ride is part of FamTap, the family app, so safety is built around the people who care about you.

  • 4-digit start codeThe trip starts only when the driver enters your code, so the right person is in the right vehicle.
  • Share with familyUp to 5 family members see the ride live: driver, number plate and where they are.
  • SOS in one tapCalls 112 and alerts your family at once, even if their app is closed.
  • Know your driverPhoto, name, Verified badge, rating, yellow plate and driver code before they arrive.
  • Help the driver find youShare your live location with the driver until pickup, and chat or call them in the app.
  • Private by designPhone numbers are shown only after a driver accepts, and only for that ride. Addresses from your rides are deleted after 30 days.

How to join

In FamTap: Settings → Become a driver. Add your vehicle details and photos of:

  1. Your photo
  2. Driving licence
  3. RC (registration)
  4. Insurance
  5. Permit
  6. ID proof
  7. Vehicle with yellow plate

FamTap checks your documents and approves you. Then Fam Ride Driver appears in your chat list.

Questions

Good to know

Where does Fam Ride work?

Fam Ride has started in Jamalpur and Munger (Bihar), with more towns coming. When you open Fam Ride, the app checks if you are inside a Fam Ride town.

How do I pay?

At the end of the trip, scan the Fam Ride QR on the driver's phone with any UPI app, or pay cash to the driver. The fare is fixed when you book.

Do I need a separate app?

No. Fam Ride is inside the FamTap app. Install FamTap from Google Play and tap Fam Ride in your chat list.

Can I book for someone else?

Yes. Choose "someone else", enter their name and phone number, and the driver picks them up. You can share the ride with family so everyone can follow it.

Is my location shared all the time?

No. Your location is used when you choose it for pickup, and shared with the driver only if you turn on "Share my live location", until pickup. The driver's location is shared during your ride.

Is Fam Ride on iPhone?

Not yet. FamTap and Fam Ride are on Android today. An iPhone version is planned.
