1. What the app does

FamTap lets families stay in touch simply: send messages, photos, videos, voice notes, documents, contacts and your location; make voice and video calls; post status updates that disappear after 24 hours; give voice commands such as "Call Mom" or "Message Gaurav"; and, if you choose, let someone you trust see and tap your screen to help you.

3. How your information is protected

  • Everything sent between the app and our server is encrypted in transit (HTTPS).
  • Voice and video calls are encrypted between the two phones. When a direct connection is not possible, the encrypted call passes through a relay that cannot read it.
  • Messages, attachments, locations, status updates, screen sharing and the Remote control camera picture are end-to-end encrypted. They are encrypted on your phone and can only be decrypted on the phones of the people you send them to. Our server stores and delivers the encrypted data but cannot read it, and neither can we.
  • The private key that decrypts your messages is created on your phone and never leaves it. If you change phone or reinstall the app, messages sent to your old phone cannot be shown on the new one. We cannot recover them for you.
  • You can compare the security code shown in a chat with the other person. If both phones show the same code, nobody is in between.
  • What we can still see: your phone number, name and "About" line, who you message or call and when, and the size of what you send.
  • This encryption uses standard, widely used methods (ECDH P-256, AES-256-GCM), but it has not yet been reviewed by an independent security auditor, and it does not yet protect past messages if a phone's key is later stolen (no "forward secrecy").
  • You sign in with a code sent by SMS; there is no password to steal. Signing in on a new phone signs your old phone out, and it is told why.
  • App data is excluded from phone backups, so your sign-in does not get copied into cloud backups.
  • Our server limits how fast any account can send messages, ring other phones or open connections, to protect people from spam and abuse.

No system is perfectly secure. If we learn of a breach affecting your information, we will tell you.

6. Deleting your account and data

You can delete your account at any time in the app: Settings → Delete my account.

This permanently deletes your account, your one-to-one chats and their messages and media (for you and for the other person), the messages and media you sent in groups, your status updates, your profile photo, your group memberships, and your block list. It cannot be undone.

If you cannot use the app, email famtap.help@gmail.com from any address with the subject "Delete my FamTap account" and tell us the phone number of the account. We will confirm it is yours and delete it within 30 days.

Logging out (Settings → Log out) is different: it removes chats saved on your phone and signs you out, but keeps your account.

7. Your choices

  • You can refuse or withdraw any permission in your phone's Settings. Calls need the microphone; finding friends needs contacts; the rest of the app keeps working.
  • You can hide your "last seen" in Settings.
  • You can block or report anyone from the chat with them.
  • You can ask us for a copy of the information we hold about you, or ask us to correct it, at famtap.help@gmail.com.

8. Children

FamTap is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. In India, people under 18 should use the app only with the consent of a parent or guardian. If you believe a child has given us information without that consent, contact us and we will delete it.
